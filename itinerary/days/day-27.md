## Day 27 — Oct 27 — Kii-Katsuura → Daimon-zaka → Kumano Nachi Taisha / Seigantoji / Nachi Falls → Kii-Katsuura

**Sleep:** Kii-Katsuura (night 2 of 2)  
**Lodging type:** same as previous night.

### Purpose

This is the proper Nachi finale of the Kumano section. Saving Nachi for this day avoids making Day 26 too rushed and makes the two-night Kii-Katsuura stay feel purposeful rather than padded.

### Plan

- Start from Kii-Katsuura in the morning  
- Bus toward Daimon-zaka / Nachi area  
- Walk **Daimon-zaka** upward if weather is good — 600m cobblestone stairway, 267 steps, ancient cedar grove  
- Visit **Kumano Nachi Taisha** — 3rd of the Kumano Sanzan, free entry  
- Visit **Seigantoji** and the pagoda viewpoint (¥300 temple, ¥100 viewing platform — iconic pagoda-and-falls view)  
- Visit **Nachi Falls** base (¥300, last entry 16:00 — Japan’s tallest single-drop waterfall at 133m)  
- Return by bus to Kii-Katsuura  
- Afternoon/evening recovery: harbor walk, tuna / seafood dinner, onsen if staying somewhere with baths

### Notes

This should be treated as the emotional finish of the Kumano section. Do not rush onward to Osaka today.

The route completes the Kumano Sanzan: Hongu on Day 25, Hayatama on Day 26, Nachi on Day 27 — a calm, coherent shrine-and-pilgrimage ending.
