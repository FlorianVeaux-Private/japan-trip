## Day 3 — Oct 3 — Meiji Jingu, Harajuku, Shibuya and Nintendo TOKYO

**Sleep:** Tokyo

This day combines a forested shrine morning with Harajuku and modern/pop-culture Tokyo.

### Plan

- **Meiji Jingu** in the morning
- **Harajuku**
- **Shibuya crossing / Hachiko**
- **Nintendo TOKYO** at Shibuya PARCO
- Optional **UNIQLO** shopping
- Casual food and evening in Shibuya or a nearby calmer neighborhood

### Recommended skeleton

**Suggested flow:**

1. Meiji Jingu
2. Harajuku / Takeshita Street
3. Shibuya crossing / Hachiko quick look
4. Shibuya PARCO
5. Nintendo TOKYO
6. UNIQLO if useful
7. Casual dinner

### Filler options depending on mood

- **Shibuya Sky** — strong paid skyline option if the weather is clear and you want one city-view experience.
- **Miyashita Park** — easy modern urban filler near Shibuya.
- **Daikanyama** — calmer cafés, bookstores and design shops if you want a softer alternative to more shopping.
- **Nakameguro** — canal-side walking, cafés and dinner options.
- **Food crawl around Shibuya/Ebisu** — ramen, standing sushi, yakitori, izakaya or dessert.

### Notes

This is not intended to be a fashion-focused day. Harajuku is worth seeing as part of the route, but do not spend hours shopping unless you find something you actually want.

If the goshuincho was not bought on Day 1, **Meiji Jingu** is another strong place to buy one.

**Booking note — Nintendo TOKYO:** Oct 3 is a **Saturday**, when Nintendo TOKYO at Shibuya PARCO uses a free **same-day numbered/timed-entry ticket** system. Tickets are handed out from roughly **8:00–9:30am** at the ground-floor entrance and can run out. Options: send one person to grab a ticket at ~8am while the other starts Meiji Jingu, do it on a weekday instead, or try walk-in entry in the evening. There is no online reservation.
