## Day 37 — Nov 6 — Miyajima morning → Fukuoka afternoon/evening

**Sleep:** Fukuoka  
**Lodging type:** city hotel near Hakata Station or Tenjin.

### Plan

Early Miyajima start, then continue west to Fukuoka. Fukuoka is now intentionally an afternoon and evening stop, not a full final city day.

**Suggested flow:**

| Time |  |
| :---- | :---- |
| 8:00am | Leave Hiroshima hotel. Leave main luggage at the hotel or use station lockers if needed. |
| 8:30am | Miyajimaguchi — JR Sanyo Line from Hiroshima Station (~25 min) |
| 8:40am | Ferry to Miyajima (~10 min). Fare ~¥200 each way + ~¥100 Miyajima visitor tax (pay out of pocket; no JR Pass on this trip). |
| 8:50am | Arrive Miyajima |
| 8:50–10:00am | **Itsukushima Shrine + torii gate** (shrine entry ~¥300) |
| 10:00am–12:00pm | **Miyajima Ropeway** up to Shishiiwa + Misen summit (535m) + ropeway back down |
| 12:00–12:30pm | **Momijidani Park** (Maple Leaf Valley) |
| 12:45pm | Ferry back to Miyajimaguchi |
| 1:30pm | Back at Hiroshima Station / hotel luggage pickup |
| 2:30–3:30pm | Shinkansen Hiroshima → Hakata / Fukuoka (~1h on Nozomi; ~1h15 on Sakura/Hikari) |
| Late afternoon | Check in to Fukuoka hotel |

> ⚠️ **Reality check on the 10:00–12:00 ropeway + summit block: this does NOT fit in 2 hours.** Shishiiwa station → Misen summit is a genuine **~30 min each-way rocky hike** (not a "short walk"), and the 2-stage ropeway gets long queues — a real summit round trip is closer to **3 hours**. I'm leaving the summit in the plan **for you to decide**: either (a) do shrine + ropeway to the **Shishiiwa observatory only** (skip the summit hike) to keep the ~2h window and protect the Fukuoka evening, or (b) commit to the summit and push the Hiroshima→Hakata shinkansen later.

**Use the ropeway both ways** — hiking up on foot takes 90 minutes and breaks the schedule.

### Fukuoka afternoon/evening

Keep the Fukuoka plan focused and low-friction.

**Best use of limited time:**

- Check in near Hakata or Tenjin  
- If there is daylight: **Tochoji Temple / Fukuoka Daibutsu** and **Kushida Shrine** in old Hakata  
- Final Japan dinner: yatai, Hakata ramen, motsunabe, yakitori, seafood, or another easy local food plan  
- Confirm ferry logistics before sleeping

### Ferry preparation

Before sleeping, confirm:

- ferry reservation  
- passport details  
- terminal location  
- check-in deadline  
- baggage rules  
- **the sailing is the daytime Camellia Line departure (~10:30, see Day 38)** — so this is your last night before an early terminal transfer  
- how to get from hotel to Hakata Port International Terminal

### Notes

Do **not** backtrack to Osaka after Miyajima — continue west to Fukuoka. The day is mainly about visiting Miyajima properly and reaching Fukuoka in time for a final evening.

Check tide tables for Nov 6 before the trip. At high tide the torii appears to float; at low tide you can walk out to the base of the gate. Plan based on which falls in the morning window.

**Deer** — roam freely around the shrine and ferry terminal. Photogenic and persistent about sniffing for food. Keep snacks sealed.

**Verify Miyajima Ropeway operational status before your trip** — it has had closure periods for maintenance. If closed: stay longer at the shrine, walk the Momijidani trail on foot, or explore the quiet residential lanes behind the shrine before the midday crowds arrive.
