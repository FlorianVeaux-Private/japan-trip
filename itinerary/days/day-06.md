## Day 6 — Oct 6 — Okunikko nature day

**Sleep:** Nikko Guesthouse Sumica  
**Reservation:** Confirmed · Oct 5–7 · [reservation ledger](../reservations.md)

### Travel: Nikko town to Okunikko

Use the local bus from Nikko toward Lake Chuzenji, Ryuzu Falls, Senjogahara and Yumoto. In autumn this route can be busy, so start early. A useful target is a **~07:50 bus from Nikko** toward Okunikko.

### Plan

This day is about nature, not just onsen.

**Possible activities:**

- Lake Chuzenji  
- Kegon Falls  
- Ryuzu Falls  
- Senjogahara marsh walk  
- Optional Yumoto Onsen stop

Do not treat every stop above as mandatory. The sensible core is **Kegon Falls / Lake Chuzenji → Ryuzu Falls → Senjogahara**; Yumoto is an optional extension depending on energy, weather and bus timing.
