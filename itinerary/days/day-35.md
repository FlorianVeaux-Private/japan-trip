## Day 35 — Nov 4 — Matsuyama → Hiroshima / Peace Memorial

**Sleep:** HOTEL MYSTAYS Hiroshima Peace Park  
**Reservation:** Confirmed · Nov 4–6 · [reservation ledger](../reservations.md)  
**Lodging type:** central city hotel near Peace Memorial Park.

### Purpose

This is a half-morning in Matsuyama followed by the focused Hiroshima experience: **Peace Memorial Park, A-Bomb Dome and the Peace Memorial Museum**. Keep the evening light so Nov 5 can be devoted entirely to Miyajima.

### Suggested Matsuyama plan

- Breakfast in central Matsuyama  
- **Matsuyama Castle** — ropeway/chairlift up from ~08:30 (or walk); castle keep opens ~09:00. An efficient ~09:00–10:30 visit works before heading to the port.  
- City views from the castle keep  
- Optional quick local lunch or tai-meshi if the ferry is around midday; otherwise keep lunch flexible in Hiroshima  
- Skip larger add-ons by default. **Ishite-ji Temple** or **Bansuiso** only fit if the ferry timing is unusually friendly.

### Travel: Matsuyama → Hiroshima

Target a **late-morning or midday fast ferry** from Matsuyama toward Hiroshima — the **"Super Jet" hydrofoil (Matsuyama Kanko Port → Hiroshima Port, ~70 min)**. Build the morning around the ferry schedule and book ahead.

**Budget the port transfer:** from central Matsuyama it's **~75–90 min door-to-boat** — Iyotetsu Takahama Line to Takahama (~15–20 min) + port shuttle/walk, plus the **30-min pre-departure check-in**. So a "midday" ferry means leaving the hotel meaningfully earlier than it sounds.

**Suggested flow:**

1. Morning for Matsuyama Castle (efficient ~09:00–10:30)  
2. Transfer to Matsuyama Kanko Port (allow 75–90 min door-to-boat)  
3. Super Jet ferry Matsuyama → Hiroshima Port (~70 min)  
4. Local transport or taxi to the hotel  
5. **Peace Memorial Park + A-Bomb Dome + Peace Memorial Museum** in the afternoon, with enough time to pause afterward  
6. Simple Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki dinner

### Notes

The Peace Museum is emotionally demanding. Do not stack another major Hiroshima sight on top. The purpose of the day is the core Hiroshima history experience; Miyajima gets its own full day tomorrow.
