## What actually needs a reservation

The trip is now mostly reservation-light. Prioritize the small number of fixed-seat or timed-entry items that materially constrain the itinerary; most sightseeing and local transport can be handled on the day.

1. **Hamakaze — Nov 1**: highest remaining transport priority because Day 32 is built around the morning Kinosaki → Himeji service and there are only a small number of southbound departures.

3. **Kuroshio — Oct 28**: reserve the Kii-Katsuura → Osaka limited express for a comfortable post-Kumano exit.
4. **Koya Limited Express — Oct 22**: reserve if you specifically want the reserved-seat Limited Express; ordinary Nankai services remain a viable fallback.
5. **Thunderbird — Oct 14**: reserve for the Kanazawa → Kyoto transfer; this is convenient rather than critical.
6. **Hiroshima → Hakata Shinkansen — Nov 6**: reserve because it connects to an international ferry; sell-out risk is low.
7. **teamLab Borderless — Oct 4**: timed admission; book ahead rather than relying on walk-up availability.
8. **Tea ceremony / one strong kaiseki meal**: reserve only once the preferred experience is chosen.
