# Decorative natural manhole covers — shortlist

The goal is to collect the particularly good **full-color decorative manhole covers** encountered along the existing route, prioritizing natural subjects (landscapes, animals, flowers) and avoiding detours solely for a manhole cover.

## Shortlist

| Day | Place | Natural motif | Location / route | Integration |
| :--: | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 6 | Nikko | Lake Chuzenji + Kegon Falls | Lake Chuzenji / Kegon Falls area | Directly on the Day 6 nature route |
| 11 | Takayama | Kobano mitsubatsutsuji flowers + foliage | Nakabashi Park | Directly between Takayama Jinya and the old town |
| 21 | Nara | Sika deer + yaezakura cherry blossoms | South of Kintetsu Nara Station | Natural start/end point for the Nara Park / Naramachi day |
| 29 | Himeji | Sagisou orchid + water | Otemae-dori, between Himeji Station and Himeji Castle | Directly on the castle approach |
| 34 | Matsuyama | Yamatsubaki camellia + Iyo-kasuri motif | Central Matsuyama / Dogo area | Opportunistic on the evening route; no dedicated detour |
| 35 | Hiroshima | Maple leaves + carp in water | Hiroshima Castle area | Optional bonus only; do not displace the Peace Memorial itinerary |

## Rules for the trip

- These are a **shortlist, not a separate sightseeing program**.
- Prioritize covers already on the day's walking route.
- Do not add a major transport detour solely for a manhole cover.
- If timing, weather or fatigue makes one inconvenient, skip it.
- The six above were selected because they combine a clearly natural motif with a location that can be attached to an existing itinerary day.

## Practical priority

**High-value / essentially free:** Nikko, Takayama, Nara, Himeji.

**Good but opportunistic:** Matsuyama.

**Optional:** Hiroshima — keep this secondary to the Peace Memorial Park / museum day.
