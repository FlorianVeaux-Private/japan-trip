## Day 33 — Nov 2 — Shimanami Kaido cycling day 1: Onomichi → Setoda / Ikuchijima

**Sleep:** Setoda / Ikuchijima  
**Lodging type:** boutique inn, guesthouse, or small hotel on the island.

### Main activity: Shimanami Kaido day 1

Pick up bikes at the Onomichi terminal early morning. The full Onomichi–Imabari cycling route is about **70 km**, split over two days to keep it scenic and relaxed rather than endurance-focused.

### Suggested route

- Pick up bikes in Onomichi  
- Ferry from Onomichi port → Mukaishima (short crossing)  
- Ride Mukaishima → Innoshima → Ikuchijima  
- Stop for views, citrus, cafés, small detours  
- Sleep around Setoda / Ikuchijima

Approximately **30–35 km**. Comfortable pace with bridge stops and sea views.

**Optional stop:** Kosanji Temple on Ikuchijima if arriving before closing — an ornate Buddhist complex built 1936–1987 by a steel tycoon, sometimes called "the Nikko of the west." Unusual, worth 1 hour if the timing works.

### Luggage

Do not bike with large luggage. Main bags should already have been forwarded from Osaka to Hiroshima before leaving for Kinosaki. Carry only the small cycling/travel bag through Kinosaki, Onomichi, the Shimanami Kaido, and the Matsuyama night.
