## Day 5 — Oct 5 — Tokyo → Nikko

**Sleep:** Nikko Guesthouse Sumica  
**Reservation:** Confirmed · Oct 5–7 · [reservation ledger](../reservations.md)

### Travel: Tokyo to Nikko

The best route is the **Tobu Limited Express SPACIA X 001 from Asakusa to Tobu-Nikko**, **07:50 → 09:39**, booked for 2 adults in standard seats (Car 4, seats 11C/11D). **Order ID: Ex202609080DF8IW.** Limited Express ticket total: **¥3,880**. The basic Tobu fare is separate. Slower rapid/local Tobu trains or JR routes (via Utsunomiya) take longer, closer to 2–2.5 hours with a connection.

### Plan — Nikko historic core + Takino forest

**1. 09:39 — Arrive at Tobu-Nikko**
- Stop at the **Nikko Tourist Center** first for maps/orientation and to handle the tickets that are useful to arrange there.
- **Toshogu:** current official price **¥1,600**; April–October hours **09:00–17:00**, last entry **16:30**.
- **Rinnoji:** Sanbutsudo + Taiyuin combo **¥900**; April–October hours **08:00–17:00**.
- Do not assume every shrine ticket is sold at the station: **Futarasan** and **Shinkyo** have their own ticketing.

**2. Shinkyo**
- Start with the bridge from the outside; the paid crossing is optional and costs **¥300**.
- October hours are currently listed as **08:30–16:30**.

**3. Rinnoji**
- **Sanbutsudo**, then **Shoyoen** if time allows.
- Keep this relatively efficient: Toshogu is the main timed visit.

**4. Toshogu**
- Allow roughly **1.5–2 h** rather than trying to race through it.
- Priorities: **Three Wise Monkeys**, **Sleeping Cat**, **Elephants**, **Yomeimon**, **Sacred Stable**, **Ieyasu's tomb**, and the **Crying Dragon**.
- The site closes earlier than the other parts of the complex, so it gets priority over the flexible late-afternoon stops.

**5. Futarasan-jinja**
- **08:00–17:00 in October**, last entry **16:30**.
- Keep the visit focused before leaving the main shrine area for Takino.

**6. Takino Jinja — forest section**
- Walk from the Futarasan/Toshogu area through the forest to **Takino Jinja**, with **Sanbon-sugi**, torii and the smaller shrines along the way.
- This is a real forest/mountain walking section, not just a paved shrine transfer: **wear proper walking shoes**.
- Use the toilets before leaving the main shrine area; facilities are much less convenient once on the forest approach.
- Look for the **Torii of Luck** on the route.
- Treat the timing as flexible: weather, trail conditions and walking pace can make this section longer than the map suggests.

**7. Lunch — local Nikko food**
- Aim for **yuba (tofu skin)** and/or **soba**.
- Yuba is one of Nikko's defining local foods, so this is a good place to make lunch part of the experience rather than just a fuel stop.

**8. Taiyuin**
- Iemitsu's mausoleum, immediately beside Futarasan.
- **08:00–17:00 in October**, last entry **16:30**; current admission **¥550**.
- Quieter and more atmospheric than Toshogu; keep it even if the day is getting tight.

**9. Tamozawa Imperial Villa**
- **09:00–17:00 in October**, last admission **16:00**; current admission **¥600**.
- This is the most important hard cutoff of the late afternoon. If the day is running late, **prioritize Tamozawa over Kanmangafuchi**.

**10. Kanmangafuchi + Bake Jizo**
- Riverside walk to the row of **Bake Jizo** statues.
- It is a lower-priority flexible finish and can be shortened or dropped if the earlier visits consume the afternoon.

**Route:** Tobu-Nikko 09:39 → Tourist Center → Shinkyo → Rinnoji → Toshogu → Futarasan → Takino Jinja → lunch → Taiyuin → Tamozawa → Kanmangafuchi → dinner.

### Evening — yuba dinner

**Nikko Yubamaki ZEN** is the preferred yuba idea, but **do not treat it as a Monday dinner option**: the restaurant's own current site lists dinner **Wednesday–Sunday and holidays, 17:00–20:30 (last order 18:30)**, with Tuesday closed. **Oct 5, 2026 is a Monday.**

Keep ZEN as a lunch option or choose another Nikko restaurant for Monday dinner; do not build the evening around a ZEN dinner reservation.

### Timing logic

This is deliberately a full Nikko day. The non-negotiable timed visits are **Toshogu** and **Tamozawa**; **Takino** is the main walking variable. If the day slips, cut **Kanmangafuchi first**, then shorten the optional parts of Rinnoji/Futarasan rather than rushing Toshogu or missing Tamozawa.

### Why overnight in Nikko

Nikko is much better as an overnight than as a rushed day trip. It gives more flexibility and makes the next day in Okunikko easier.
