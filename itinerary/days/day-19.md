## Day 19 — Oct 19 — Uji + Nintendo Museum

**Sleep:** Kyoto

Combine central Uji with the fixed afternoon museum booking. **Skip the Tale of Genji Museum**; the day is already full enough without it.

### Morning and early afternoon — central Uji

Kyoto → Uji is an easy local train trip. Depending on the route and Kyoto departure point, allow roughly **20–40 minutes** each way. Aim to arrive around **09:00**.

Suggested order:

1. Visit **Byodoin Temple** first. If you want the timed Phoenix Hall interior visit, check its assigned time on arrival and make sure it does not conflict with the tea ceremony.
2. **11:30 — Taihoan traditional tea service (booked for 2 people).** Booked option: **usucha (薄茶, usucha; matcha)**, approximately 20–30 minutes. Exchange the booking confirmation for the actual ticket at the Uji City Tourist Information Center opposite Taihoan before the session; wear socks for the tatami room.
3. Have lunch, then walk along the **Uji River** and visit **Ujigami Shrine**.
4. Browse matcha / tea shops. **Menya Inoichi's Uji branch (Menya Inoichi #3)** is an option, around 5 minutes from Uji Station.
5. Collect the two central Uji Poké Lids:
   - Scorbunny / Spinda — **34.890627, 135.809787**
   - Poltchageist / Sinistcha — **34.895547, 135.805962**

Keep the river/shrine walk and shopping flexible rather than trying to fit in another museum. Leave central Uji around **15:00** to travel to Ogura with a comfortable margin before the museum booking.

### Afternoon — Nintendo Museum

- **16:00 — confirmed booking.**
- The museum is near Ogura Station, separate from Uji's central sightseeing area. Aim to reach the area around **15:40–15:45** and follow the museum's check-in requirements.
- Allow the museum its own visit time; do not add another major attraction afterward.

### Poké Lid — Nintendo Museum

Collect the **Pikachu Poké Lid — 34.892561, 135.784525** during the museum visit; museum admission is required.
