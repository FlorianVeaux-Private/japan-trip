## Day 26 — Oct 26 — Yunomine / Hongu → Shingu / Kumano Hayatama Taisha → Kii-Katsuura

**Sleep:** Kii-Katsuura (night 1 of 2)  
**Lodging type:** seaside hotel, onsen hotel, or practical station-area hotel.

### Purpose

This is a transition day from the Hongu/Yunomine onsen area to the eastern coast. It completes the second Kumano Sanzan shrine without rushing Nachi.

### Plan

* Relaxed morning in **Yunomine Onsen**  
* Optional soak in **Tsuboyu**, the historic UNESCO-listed private bath (individual bathing rooms are reserved on a first-come, first-served basis, with short timed sessions)  
* Stroll through the village before departure  
* Mid-morning bus from Yunomine / Hongu toward Shingu  
* Stop in Shingu for **Kumano Hayatama Taisha** — vermilion pavilions, 1,000-year-old Nagi tree, allow about 1 hour  
* Continue by train or bus to Kii-Katsuura  
* Check in  
* Tuna / seafood dinner  
* Onsen or harbor evening

### Notes

This is a deliberately calm day. It should not include Nachi Falls — Hayatama Taisha is the Shingu stop, and Nachi is saved for Day 27.

If timing and legs are excellent after Hayatama, **Kamikura-jinja** can be added in Shingu — a dramatic staircase shrine above the city — but it should not be part of the default plan because Nachi is still ahead the next day.

The first Kii-Katsuura night is useful because it avoids compressing Hayatama and Nachi into the same day.
