### Travel: Tokyo to Nikko

The best route is the **Tobu Limited Express Spacia X from Asakusa to Tobu-Nikko**, roughly **1h50 direct** — reserve seats in advance (Spacia X sells out). A useful morning departure is **07:50 → 09:39**. Slower rapid/local Tobu trains or JR routes (via Utsunomiya) take longer, closer to 2–2.5 hours with a connection.

### Plan

Travel early enough to arrive before midday or early afternoon.

**Main activities:**

- Toshogu Shrine  
- Rinnoji Temple  
- Futarasan Shrine  
- Shinkyo Bridge  
- Forested shrine approaches  
- **Taiyuin** — Iemitsu's mausoleum, right next to Futarasan; quieter and arguably more beautiful than Toshogu (easy low-effort add)  
- **Kanmangafuchi Abyss** — riverside row of Jizo statues, strong autumn scenery and low crowds (\~30 min walk from the shrine area)

**Why overnight in Nikko:**  
Nikko is much better as an overnight than as a rushed day trip. It gives more flexibility and makes the next day in Okunikko easier.
