> ### ⚠️ Book these Kyoto experiences well ahead (peak autumn)

> The 5 open Kyoto base days are fine as placeholders, but several first-timer highlights are reservation-gated and can't be arranged on the day:  
> 

> - **Saihoji (Kokedera moss temple)** — online reservation opens **exactly 2 months ahead** (≈ Aug 20 for an Oct 20 visit).  
> - **A top kaiseki dinner** — the best places book weeks ahead, more so in foliage season.  
> - **A formal tea ceremony** — reserve ahead.  
> - **Arashiyama bamboo grove** — go at opening to beat crowds (no booking, but timing matters).

> ### 🎮 Nintendo Museum — fixed reservation

> - Near **Ogura Station** (between Kyoto and Uji), ~2–4 hours on its own.  
> - **Booked:** **Mon Oct 19 at 16:00**. Treat this as a fixed afternoon reservation and build Day 19 around it.  
> - **Closed Tuesdays** — so Oct 20 is not an option.
