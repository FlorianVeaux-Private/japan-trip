**Important note:**  
Do not hike today. This day exists to make the Kumano section smoother and avoid starting the trail tired.

**Why Kii-Tanabe:**  
Kii-Tanabe is the practical gateway to the Nakahechi route. Sleeping here makes it possible to take a morning bus to Takijiri-oji and start the pilgrimage properly the next day.
