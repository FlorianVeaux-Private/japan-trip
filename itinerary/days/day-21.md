## Day 21 — Oct 21 — Kyoto → Nara overnight

**Sleep:** Ryokan Asukasou at the entrance of Nara Park  
**Reservation:** Confirmed · Oct 21–22 · [reservation ledger](../reservations.md)  
**Lodging type:** ryokan.

### Travel: Kyoto to Nara

Kyoto → Nara is easy by JR or Kintetsu train. Kintetsu Nara Station is usually more convenient for Nara Park and the old town area. Expect roughly **35–50 minutes** depending on route.

### Plan

- Travel Kyoto → Nara in the morning  
- Todai-ji  
- Nigatsu-do  
- Kasuga Taisha  
- Nara Park  
- Evening in Naramachi after day-trippers leave

**Why overnight in Nara:**  
Nara is more atmospheric early and late. Staying overnight helps avoid the day-trip crowd rhythm.

**Time reality:** you arrive Nara around midday and leave for Koyasan ~9am the next morning, so this is really **one afternoon + one evening** (not a full day). Todai-ji, Nigatsu-do and Kasuga Taisha are a 20–30 min walk apart across the park — doable but not leisurely. Either arrive as early as possible, or treat anything beyond those three + a Naramachi evening as optional.
