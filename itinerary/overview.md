# Japan 5-Week Itinerary — Tokyo to Busan

**Dates:** October 1 to November 7  
**Direction:** Tokyo arrival → Hiroshima / Hakata ferry departure to Busan  
**Travel style:** traditional culture, food, beautiful nature, old roads, light hiking, limited rural complexity

---

## Overview

This itinerary is built around a first trip to Japan that includes the classic cultural core, but avoids being only the Golden Route. It combines Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka and Nara with Nikko, the Kiso Valley, Takayama, Kanazawa, Koyasan, a Kumano Kodo taster, Kinosaki Onsen, Onomichi, the Shimanami Kaido, Matsuyama, Hiroshima, Miyajima, and the final ferry from Hakata to Busan.

The trip is full but coherent. The most logistically demanding stretch is the middle-to-late section:

**Koyasan → Nakahechi / Hongu → Shingu / Hayatama → Nachi / Kii-Katsuura → Osaka → Kinosaki Onsen → Onomichi → Shimanami Kaido → Matsuyama → Hiroshima → Miyajima → Hakata → Busan ferry**

This is also one of the most distinctive parts of the trip. There is an Osaka recovery block after Kumano, then Kinosaki as a one-night onsen-town reset before the Shimanami cycling section. Matsuyama is a one-night Shikoku landing after the ride: enough time for Matsuyama Castle and the Seto Inland Sea ferry. Hiroshima gets a focused Peace Memorial day, followed by a full Miyajima day before the Nov 6 ferry from Hakata to Busan.

In Kumano, the route completes the Kumano Sanzan by walking the Nakahechi to Hongu, then visiting Hayatama Taisha in Shingu and giving Nachi its own day from a two-night Kii-Katsuura base.

The trip finishes westward — Shimanami Kaido → Matsuyama → Hiroshima → Miyajima → Hakata → Busan ferry — without backtracking to Osaka.
