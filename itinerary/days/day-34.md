## Day 34 — Nov 3 (Culture Day) — Shimanami Kaido cycling day 2: Hakata-jima → Imabari → Matsuyama

**Sleep:** Matsuyama  
**Lodging type:** comfortable central Matsuyama hotel (near Matsuyama-shi / Okaido or the JR station).

### Why Culture Day works for cycling

National holiday = virtually no commercial trucks on the bridges, lighter traffic, more Japanese families out cycling. The dedicated cycling paths are unchanged. The atmosphere is more festive.

### Main activity: Shimanami Kaido day 2

**Suggested route:**

- Hakata-jima → Oshima  
- Oshima → Imabari via the Kurushima Kaikyo Bridge — one of the route's most dramatic crossings, with views of the famous tidal straits  
- Continue approximately **6 km from Sunrise Itoyama to Imabari Station**  
- Return bikes at the **i.i.imabari! Cycle Station beside JR Imabari Station**

Approximately **30–35 km**. The shorter second day is intentional: after a longer Day 33, this leaves a more comfortable margin for the bike return and Matsuyama transfer. The final ~6 km to Imabari Station is ordinary city/public-road riding rather than another scenic Shimanami section, but it is straightforward and avoids a separate bus/taxi transfer.

### Travel: Imabari → Matsuyama

After returning the bikes at **i.i.imabari! Cycle Station beside JR Imabari Station**, take the **JR Yosan Line limited express (Shiokaze / Ishizuchi) to Matsuyama, ~40 min**. The station-side bike return means there is no separate transfer from the cycling terminal; check the departure before finishing the ride and keep a reasonable buffer for the bike return.

### Evening in Matsuyama

This is a soft landing after the Shimanami ride, not a sightseeing sprint. The goal is simply to arrive, recover and eat well — expect low energy after the ride plus the transfer, so don't force any activity on arrival.

**Suggested flow:**

- Check in and recover from the ride  
- Easy stroll around central Matsuyama (Okaido / Gintengai arcades)  
- Dinner: tai-meshi or local Ehime seafood  
- Early night

### Notes

Do **not** continue to Hiroshima tonight. Sleeping in Matsuyama avoids a punishing Shimanami → Hiroshima transfer and keeps Hiroshima, Miyajima, and Fukuoka protected.

Because Nov 3 is Culture Day, book the Matsuyama lodging early and reserve any transport that allows reservations.
