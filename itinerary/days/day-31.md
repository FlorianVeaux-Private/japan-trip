**Sleep:** Kinosaki Onsen  
**Lodging type:** ryokan or ryokan-style inn, ideally including dinner and breakfast.

### Travel: Osaka to Kinosaki Onsen

Use the JR limited express **Kounotori** from Shin-Osaka toward **Kinosaki Onsen** — **~2h45** (note only ~6 of the daily Kounotori run all the way to Kinosaki; others terminate at Fukuchiyama and need a transfer). Reserve seats in advance.

This is also the day to send main luggage onward.

**Recommended luggage move:**  
Forward main bags from **Osaka → the Hiroshima hotel** before leaving Osaka. Carry only a small bag for:

- Kinosaki Onsen  
- Onomichi  
- Shimanami Kaido cycling  
- Matsuyama

**You are only in Hiroshima on Nov 4**, so reunite with the main luggage there **on Nov 4** (after the Matsuyama → Hiroshima ferry), not Nov 3. Because that's ~3–4 days after shipping, do this right:

- The **Hiroshima hotel must be booked before you ship** (the label needs the exact hotel name/address + your name).  
- **Tell the hotel in advance** they'll receive and hold a package several days before check-in (routine in Japan, but confirm).  
- With Yamato, **set a scheduled delivery date** (~Nov 3–4) rather than the default next-day, so the bags don't arrive days before you.  
- Keep the tracking number.

### Purpose

The goal here is not a sightseeing-heavy stop but a proper reset between the Kumano/Kii Peninsula section and the cycling/western-Japan finale.

Kinosaki is useful here because it is compact, atmospheric, and works well as a one-night stay: arrive, change into yukata, walk the canal streets, visit public bathhouses, eat ryokan dinner, and sleep early.

### Plan

- Morning or late-morning departure from Osaka  
- Train to Kinosaki Onsen  
- Arrive early afternoon if possible  
- Drop bags / check in  
- Walk the willow-lined canal area  
- Start the **sotoyu-meguri** public bathhouse circuit  
- Ryokan dinner  
- Evening bathhouse stroll in yukata

### Notes

A one-night Kinosaki stay works, but only if the day is protected. Do not add Amanohashidate, Tottori, or any major detour by default.

This is slightly onsen-heavy after Yunomine / Kii-Katsuura, but it is a different kind of onsen experience: Yunomine is pilgrimage-context onsen, while Kinosaki is the classic public-bath onsen town experience.
