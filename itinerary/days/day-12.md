## Day 12 — Oct 12 — Takayama → Kanazawa (direct)

**Sleep:** Hotel Nikko Kanazawa  
**Reservation:** Confirmed · Oct 12–14 · [reservation ledger](../reservations.md)  
**Lodging type:** comfortable city hotel, machiya-style stay, or refined inn.

### Travel: Takayama to Kanazawa (direct)

Two options:

1. **Nohi / Hokutetsu express highway bus Takayama → Kanazawa** — ~2h15 direct, **reservation recommended** (reserve via nouhibus.co.jp or the Nohi bus centre; book early in this festival week).  
2. **Train:** Hida Limited Express Takayama → Toyama (~1h30) + Hokuriku Shinkansen Toyama → Kanazawa (~25 min). More frequent, a bit pricier.

It's a **short morning transfer**, so you arrive Kanazawa around midday and get a real half-day there on top of the full Day 13.

### Plan

- Late-morning transfer from Takayama to Kanazawa (bus or train)  
- Check in / drop bags  
- Afternoon: first Kanazawa sights (e.g. Higashi Chaya district or Omicho Market)  
- Seafood dinner in Kanazawa

**Note:**  
There is no Shirakawa-go stop — it overlaps with the Kiso Valley/Takayama "preserved mountain village" register, and skipping it buys more time in Kanazawa.
