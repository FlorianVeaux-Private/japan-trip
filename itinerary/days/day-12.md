## Day 12 — Oct 12 — Takayama → Kanazawa (direct)

**Sleep:** Kanazawa

### Travel: Takayama to Kanazawa (direct)

Take a **direct express highway bus** from Takayama to Kanazawa (~2h15). Reserve if possible. The train via Toyama is the fallback if the bus timing does not work.

**Shirakawa-go is deliberately dropped.** The goal is to reach Kanazawa around midday and have more time there rather than turning the transfer into a sightseeing conveyor belt.

### Plan

- Morning in Takayama if time allows  
- Direct bus to Kanazawa  
- Check in / drop bags  
- Omicho Market  
- Higashi Chaya District  
- Dinner around Katamachi or Kiguramachi
