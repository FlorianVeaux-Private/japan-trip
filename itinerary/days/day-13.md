## Day 13 — Oct 13 — Kanazawa full day

**Sleep:** Kanazawa

You have a **Day 12 afternoon plus this full day** in Kanazawa. Focus on the best of Kanazawa.  
**++ Gold leaf workshop?** — a gold-leaf workshop runs ~60–90 min and clusters near Higashi Chaya; it fits if you tighten the morning, but you'll likely need to drop one other stop (e.g. Nagamachi or the 21st Century Museum) rather than add it on top.

**Suggested plan:**

- Omicho Market early  
- Kenrokuen Garden  
- Kanazawa Castle Park  
- Higashi Chaya district  
- Nagamachi samurai district if energy allows  
- Seafood, sushi, sake, or kaiseki dinner

**Optional swaps:**

- 21st Century Museum if you want an art dose  
- Myoryuji / Ninja Temple if booked in advance

**Why 2 nights is enough here:**  
Kanazawa is refined and rewarding, but its core sights are compact. A midday arrival + afternoon (Day 12) plus one full day (Day 13) is a good fit for this itinerary.
