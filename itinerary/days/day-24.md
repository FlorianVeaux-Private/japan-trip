## Day 24 — Oct 24 — Kii-Tanabe → Takijiri-oji → Chikatsuyu / Tsugizakura area

**Sleep:** 民宿 和合 Minshuku WAGO — **booked via booking.com**  
**Lodging type:** minshuku, guesthouse, small ryokan, or walkers’ lodging.

### Travel: Kii-Tanabe to Takijiri-oji

Take the morning local bus from Kii-Tanabe to **Takijiri-oji**, the traditional-feeling start of the Nakahechi route.

Check the bus timetable carefully and build the morning around it.

### Main hike: Takijiri-oji → Takahara → Chikatsuyu / Tsugizakura area

This is the first real walking day of the Kumano Kodo section.

The goal is to begin at Takijiri-oji rather than skipping directly to the easier Hongu approach. This makes the route feel like a genuine pilgrimage rather than a collection of disconnected highlights.

### Plan

- Bus from Kii-Tanabe to Takijiri-oji  
- Visit Kumano Kodo Kan Pilgrimage Center if timing allows  
- Start walking from Takijiri-oji  
- Climb toward Takahara  
- Continue through the Nakahechi countryside and forest sections  
- Sleep around Chikatsuyu, Nonaka, or Tsugizakura-oji depending on lodging availability

### Notes

This is a meaningful hiking day, not just a short scenic walk. Start early, carry water and snacks, and keep the pace steady.

**Lodging priority:**  
Book this night early. The exact sleeping location will determine the next day’s distance.
