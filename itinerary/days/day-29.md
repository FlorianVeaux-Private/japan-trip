## Day 29 — Oct 29 — Himeji day trip from Osaka

**Sleep:** Osaka

### Travel: Osaka to Himeji

Take the direct JR Kobe Line Special Rapid from Osaka Station (around **1 hour**). Leave Hotel ASIATO around **06:45–07:00** and target a train departing Osaka Station around **07:15–07:30**. Check the live departure board or route planner that morning; this is a target window, not a booked train.

### Plan

- Arrive at Himeji around **08:15–08:30**
- Walk north along Otemae-dori towards the castle; look for the coloured **Sagisou orchid + water manhole**
- **09:00–11:45 — Himeji Castle**, including the main keep
- **11:45–12:30 — Lunch** near the castle
- **12:30–14:00 — Koko-en Garden**
- **14:00–15:00 — Walk back to Himeji Station**
- Target a return train around **14:30–15:00**, reaching Osaka around **15:30–16:15**
- Optional: Umeda Sky Building for sunset if the weather and energy are good
- Dinner in Osaka

### Decorative manhole cover — Himeji

Himeji's colored cover uses the **Sagisou orchid and water**. The city says colored versions are installed on **Otemae-dori**, the main axis between Himeji Station and the castle, so this is effectively free: keep an eye out during the station → castle walk rather than making a separate stop.

**Why Himeji is a strong addition:**  
It adds one of Japan’s great castle experiences without adding a new overnight stop. It balances the itinerary nicely.
