## Day 9 — Oct 9 — Magome → Tsumago Nakasendo walk

**Sleep:** Magome - さかのみち-sakanomichi

### Main activity: Magome → Tsumago walk

Walk the preserved Nakasendo section in the traditional direction — mostly downhill, \~8km, 2-3 hours at a comfortable pace.

| Time |  |
| :---- | :---- |
| \~08:00 | Start directly from **Magome**. |
| \~11:00 | Arrive **Tsumago**. Lunch in the post town. |
| Afternoon | Slow wander through Tsumago — town quiets down after \~4pm |
| \~15:40 | Target bus Tsumago → Nagiso (\~10 min), giving a comfortable connection margin |
| \~16:26 | Train Nagiso → Nakatsugawa (\~20 min) |
| Afternoon / evening | Return from Nakatsugawa to **Magome** by bus; exact connection depends on the day and should be checked against the current local timetable |

The Nagiso → Nakatsugawa local train runs roughly hourly — check the exact departure and leave Tsumago with enough margin. The current timetable has a **16:10 Tsumago → 16:18 Nagiso** bus followed by a **16:26 Nagiso → 16:45 Nakatsugawa** train, but the 15:40 bus is preferable because it avoids relying on an 8-minute connection.

If the Tsumago → Nagiso bus is missed, take the next service rather than trying to force the connection; the onward train runs roughly hourly. Returning to Magome by bus after Nakatsugawa is the final leg of the day.

**Why this is a key module:**  
It gives traditional architecture, old-road history and forest walking without making the trip a trekking expedition.
