## Day 19 — Oct 19 — Uji + Nintendo Museum

**Sleep:** Kyoto

Combine central Uji with the fixed afternoon museum booking. **Skip the Tale of Genji Museum**; the day is already full enough without it.

### Morning and early afternoon — central Uji

Kyoto → Uji is an easy local train trip. Depending on the route and Kyoto departure point, allow roughly **20–40 minutes** each way.

Suggested order:

1. Take a morning train to Uji.
2. Visit **Byodoin Temple**.
3. Walk along the **Uji River** and visit **Ujigami Shrine**.
4. Browse matcha / tea shops and have lunch in Uji. **Menya Inoichi's Uji branch (Menya Inoichi #3)** is an option, around 5 minutes from Uji Station.
5. Collect the two central Uji Poké Lids:
   - Scorbunny / Spinda — **34.890627, 135.809787**
   - Poltchageist / Sinistcha — **34.895547, 135.805962**

A tea ceremony is optional only if a suitable slot fits comfortably. Do not squeeze it in at the expense of the museum transfer.

### Afternoon — Nintendo Museum

- **16:00 — confirmed booking.**
- The museum is near Ogura Station, separate from Uji's central sightseeing area. Leave central Uji with enough margin to reach the museum and follow its check-in requirements.
- Allow the museum its own visit time; do not add another major attraction afterward.

### Poké Lid — Nintendo Museum

Collect the **Pikachu Poké Lid — 34.892561, 135.784525** during the museum visit; museum admission is required.
