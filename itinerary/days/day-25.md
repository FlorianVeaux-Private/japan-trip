### Main hike: Chikatsuyu / Tsugizakura area → Hongu

Default plan: **no bus/taxi positioning** — walk a longer, continuous Nakahechi stage toward **Kumano Hongu Taisha** on foot. This is the longest walking day, and reaching Hongu mostly on foot is what makes the section feel like a genuine pilgrimage rather than a set of transport hops.

**Energy-dependent escape (decide on the day):** there is a fork later in the stage where a southern variant peels off and drops **directly down to Yunomine Onsen** instead of continuing to Hongu Taisha. If legs/daylight are running short (late-October sunset ~17:00), take that variant, soak at Yunomine, and simply **visit Hongu Taisha the next morning** on the way out. Keep the full Hongu-on-foot route as the goal, this as the shorter fallback. (Confirm the exact fork/signage on the day — likely the route down toward Yunomine rather than the final Hosshinmon→Hongu descent.)

### Plan

- Early breakfast at lodging (aim to start ~06:30–07:00)  
- Walk east along the Nakahechi route  
- **Either** continue to the Hongu area → visit Kumano Hongu Taisha → Oyunohara torii if timing/energy allow → short transfer to Yunomine  
- **Or** take the southern variant straight down to Yunomine Onsen (save Hongu Taisha for the next morning)  
- Onsen bath and ryokan/minshuku dinner

### Notes

Longest walking day — the full Chikatsuyu/Tsugizakura → Hongu stage is long (well over 20 km if walked in full), so start early and keep the southern-variant fallback in mind.

This is the last major Nakahechi walking day; from Day 26 the section shifts from hiking to shrine-and-coast travel via Shingu.
